The structures named, and why the cheapest repayment can mislead

Three equipment paths are named: chattel mortgage, finance lease and hire purchase. Under a chattel mortgage, the borrower owns the asset from day one, may claim GST upfront and may benefit from depreciation deductions. Under a finance lease, the lender owns the asset while the borrower leases it, GST is charged on repayments, and the asset may be returned or purchased at the end of the term. Hire purchase is named as another available path, with detail to confirm against the asset and accounting advice.

The trap is comparing only the first repayment. Ownership, GST timing, depreciation, end-of-term options and accountant input can change the real cost. A lower monthly figure may be less suitable once the final payment, balloon, tax treatment and balance-sheet effect are included.
